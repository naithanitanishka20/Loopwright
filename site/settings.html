Settings

Every rule reading the solver uses, with its default. Where the organisers' brief left a question open, the default is our reading, recorded with its question number in docs/assumptions.md; answers from the Eval-01 review are marked there.

Change the door rule in the app, or the door rule and both caps on the command line (--zone-link, --max-area, --max-nodes). The rest are set in loopwright/config.py.

Zones

zone_linkstrict

How a zone's rooms must be joined: strict through doors between themselves, circulation also through halls, bathrooms and stairs, through_rooms through any room, including another zone's.

Q1. In the app, or --zone-link.
max_area_m2100

The most floor area one hub may serve, in square metres.

The brief's cap. --max-area.
max_nodes5

The most nodes one ring may visit.

Q4, confirmed at Eval-01. --max-nodes.
pass_throughcirculation, bathroom, stair, other_zones

Spaces a ring's cable may pass through. Routing always tries without bathrooms and stairs first and uses them only when there is no other way, reporting it.

Q8, Eval-01.
living_piecesmerge

A living room ResPlan draws in pieces counts as one room.

Q9.

The cost J

bend_m0.3

Metres of cost per bend: J = length + 0.3 × bends + 3 × crossings + 2 × overlap.

Q2.
crossing_m3

Metres of cost per crossing of two cables.

Q2.
overlap_factor2

Cost per metre where cable runs over cable.

Q2.
bend_at_nodetrue

A turn at a node or at the hub counts as a bend, as on the organisers' slide 7.

Q3, slide 7.
count_self_crossingstrue

Crossings and overlap count inside one ring as well as between rings; a stretch covered k times counts k − 1 times its length.

Q3, slide 7.
tolerance_m0.001

Points or lines within this distance, in metres, count as the same.

Q3, slide 7.

Nodes and hubs

node_rulenear_doors

Where each room's node goes: near_doors, the point closest to the room's doors that keeps clear of its walls, or pole, the point farthest from the walls.

Q6, Eval-01.
node_wall_m0.5

How far a node keeps from its room's walls, in metres.

Q6, Eval-01. Checked by V15.
min_room_m22

A room smaller than this, or too narrow to hold a node that far from its walls, gets no node, and the design says so.

Q17, Eval-01.
hub_mountfree

A hub may stand anywhere inside an eligible room of its own zone.

Q7, Eval-01.
min_hub_node_m0.5

How far a hub keeps from every node of its ring, in metres.

Q7, Eval-01. Checked by V16.

Routing

separate_lanestrue

After the rings are built, legs move to a lane of their own where that lowers J, instead of running over cable already placed.

A solver option, not a rule reading.
clearance_m0.1

How far cable keeps from walls while it is routed, in metres.

A geometry constant.
axis_framedrawing

"Horizontal and vertical" means along the drawing's own axes, even beside an angled wall.

input_formatauto

Q5, Eval-01.
area_sourcepolygon

Q14.
infeasible_outputflag_and_relaxed

When no design can meet the rules, for example a room over 100 m², the plan is flagged infeasible and a relaxed design marked invalid comes with it.

Q11.
time_limit_s10

The most time one plan may take, in seconds.

Q12.
sliver_m20.5

Pieces of geometry smaller than this, in square metres, are dropped as drawing noise.
