## Blind drill 2: the train split

The first run of the train split (13,053 plans) left 4 invalid designs. Two causes, each fixed with a failing test first:

- **The scale in the output was rounded.** `frame.m_per_unit` was written rounded to 6 decimals, and to 3 in the JSON file, but the validator rebuilds every area and length from it. Plan 15080's 99.9997 m² zone read as just over 100 m². Plan 7672, valid when solved, failed V03 at 101.1 m² when validated from its file. The scale is now written exactly. The bug dates from Phase 2.
- **Thin walls on the fallback grid.** Zones the normal grid can't connect are retried on a 0.1 m grid. With no clearance, an edge could jump a wall thinner than half a grid step when both its ends and its midpoint were clear (plans 529, 9313 and 12736). Every candidate edge on that grid is now checked whole. Those three plans now get valid designs, each with one more hub, because two of their rooms can't be joined without crossing that wall.

Re-run of all 17,000 plans afterwards: every feasible plan in every split is valid. Against the first full run, only those four plans changed; on 20 others the cable length moved by ±0.1 m in the rounding, now that metres come from the exact scale.

## Second ranking key: J

J on the test split is 90% cable length and 10% bends; crossings and overlap between rings are already zero on every valid plan, so negotiated rerouting would gain nothing. The gain came from the hub instead:

- **Hub on its own ring.** For a zone with two or more nodes the hub sits on the cheapest tour through the zone's nodes, inside one of the zone's rooms, so it adds no cable; on a corner of the tour it also removes that bend, since turns at the hub don't count. Test split: cable 56,046 m → 55,415 m, bends 21,552 → 20,298, **J 62,512 → 61,504 (−1.6%)**, 1,408 plans better and none worse, hubs and validity unchanged.

## Delivered

- **Plan builder** (`loopwright/io/sketch.py`): walls, doors and boundary from room outlines in metres and the pairs of rooms that share a door. The drawing editor and both importers feed it.
- **Plan edits** (`loopwright/io/edits.py`): remove a door (its wall is filled), connect two adjacent rooms with a door, change a room's type. On plan 16448, connecting two bedrooms takes the strict rule from 4 hubs to 3.
- **Cut-the-cable analysis** (`loopwright/output/resilience.py`): for every leg, which devices are fed forward and which from the other end once it is cut, the distance to each, and the longest feed.
- **Installer pack** (`loopwright/output/installer.py`): `loopwright solve` also writes `<id>.installer.html` and `.csv`, with the drawing, a cable schedule per ring (metres with slack, bends, doors passed, loop resistance, worst feed), a bill of materials, a cost estimate and the commissioning sheet in loop address order.
- **Why this many hubs** (`loopwright/output/explain.py`): area cap, node cap, one zone, rooms the zone rule strands, or a room over the area cap.
- **DXF import** (`loopwright/io/dxf.py`): rooms on a `ROOMS` layer with a type label inside each, doors on a `DOORS` layer, units from `$INSUNITS`.
- **Photo and PDF import** (`loopwright/io/image.py`): walls by threshold with thin lines removed, door gaps closed along each wall's own direction, rooms from enclosed regions, scale from metres per pixel, the drawing ratio or the flat's area. Room types are guessed and flagged for checking.
- **Local server** (`loopwright serve`, `loopwright/api/app.py`): solve, edit, validate, import and installer pack over HTTP on 127.0.0.1, hardened against DNS rebinding, oversized uploads and image bombs.
- **Demo app**: plan by id, upload or draw on a grid; edit mode, cut-cable mode, zone-rule switch, undo, installer pack, design download.
- **Repair pass** in the solver: rooms the router can't join stop sharing a hub (`UNROUTABLE_LINK`), and places the validator rejects are avoided on the next routing attempt (`REROUTED`). It runs only when something fails.
- **Router size guard**: a plan over about 200 m by 200 m (a drawing in the wrong units) is refused at once instead of hanging the router.
- **Results table** (`loopwright results`).

## Run

```bash
python -m pip install -e ".[dev,import,serve,e2e]"
loopwright serve                                       # the app on http://127.0.0.1:8000/
loopwright solve --id 16448 --out out/                  # JSON, SVG, viewer and installer pack
loopwright batch --split val --workers 6 --report bench/val-split.md
loopwright results bench/test-split.jsonl bench/val-split.jsonl

```
